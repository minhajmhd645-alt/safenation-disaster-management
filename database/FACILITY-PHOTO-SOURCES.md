## Result

- Seven distinct photographs of the named hospitals installed; descriptions and visual contents checked, not filenames alone.

- Generated/generic photographs are no longer used on this page. Existing image assets remain in place for unrelated pages; no files or database records were removed.

## Files, reuse and maintenance

Photos: `assets/images/facilities/`. Metadata: `config/facility-photos.json`. Renderer: `app/facility_photos.php`.
Images retain the source licences linked above, including ShareAlike where applicable. Credit and licence links appear with each photograph. Public-domain source descriptions identify U.S. government photographs; the Commons source pages document the rights basis.
Only size reduction, orientation normalization and WebP conversion were performed. No AI generation, retouching, compositing, overlays baked into pixels or content replacement. The full frame is displayed with `object-fit: contain`. Responsive variants are at most 480 and 960 pixels wide; no small original was enlarged. Both variants total approximately 1.37 MiB across all ten photos; lazy loading and responsive selection reduce page transfers.
If a facility is renamed, changes district/type/source identity, or its image asset is missing, the renderer falls back to the neutral placeholder. New records also use that placeholder until a suitable licensed photograph is reviewed and entered into the manifest.
For unresolved locations, obtain a team-owned photograph or written reuse permission from the relevant hospital. Do not substitute another hospital’s photograph or interpret a whole website’s text licence as permission for every image.
